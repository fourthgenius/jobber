Job Hunter Autofill v2 (Chrome extension)

Install once (2 minutes):
1. Open Chrome and go to chrome://extensions
2. Turn on "Developer mode" (top right)
3. Click "Load unpacked" and pick the chrome-extension folder inside your job-hunter folder
   (Already installed v1? Just click the reload icon on its card instead.)
4. Click the puzzle icon in the toolbar and pin "Job Hunter Autofill"

What it does:
- On Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, SmartRecruiters, Workable and other common portals,
  it fills your details, attaches your CV and writes a cover letter for the role automatically.
- Any other site: click the extension icon > "Fill this page" (or press Alt+Shift+F).
- "Copy cover letter" in the popup copies a letter for the job on screen, to paste anywhere.
- Works even when the Job Hunter app is closed (uses snapshot.json in this folder, refreshed
  every time the app starts or you save answers).
- It never presses Submit. Check the orange fields and submit yourself; it logs the job as applied.

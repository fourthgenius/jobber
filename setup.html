Set up your job search

Tell Job Hunter what you're looking for. Everything stays on your computer, in config.json, profile.json and cv.md next to the app.
Welcome! Pick a role preset below, adjust the details, add your CV, then press Save. You can come back and change anything later.

1. What jobs do you want?

Start from a preset, then edit the lists. One item per line or comma-separated. Matching is case-insensitive on the job title.
What gets typed into job sites, e.g. "data analyst". Keep it to 3-6 terms: each one is a separate search.

2. Where?

Add the cities you'd work in. "Also matches" catches spellings and nearby areas; "Search as" is what's sent to LinkedIn/Indeed.
Used for Indeed's country site and remote searches.

e.g. "us only", "eu only" when you can't work from there.

3. Pay and experience

Jobs below your pay floor or asking too many years aren't deleted: they're marked "below bar" and hidden by default. Jobs with no salary listed are kept.

4. Your skills and interests

Used for the CV fit score and to rank jobs. Words that appear in a job description raise its score.
One per line as word: weight (1-3), e.g. "fintech: 3".

5. Where to search

All sources read public listings only. No account is used anywhere.
Company career portals: add companies under "company_boards" in the Advanced section (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workable slugs).

6. You, for application forms

Batch apply and the Chrome extension fill forms with these. Cover letters and outreach drafts use your name, headline and top achievement.

7. Your CV

Paste your CV as plain text or markdown (# Name, ## Sections, - bullets). It powers fit scoring, tailored CVs, cover letters and interview prep. Then upload the PDF you want attached to applications.

8. AI and alerts (optional)

Everything works without AI. With a Claude API key you also get AI fit scores, written cover notes, tailored CVs, interview briefs and mock-answer feedback. Set the key and Telegram daily alerts on Copilot → Setup.

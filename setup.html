    Where should Jobber look?

    The defaults work well. Every source reads public listings only: no account or login is used anywhere.

    Want specific companies? Add their career portals later under Finish → Advanced ("company_boards"), or star them in Copilot → Radar.

    Your details for application forms

    Fill these once and Jobber's Chrome extension types them into application forms for you. Leave blank anything you'd rather type yourself.

    Add your CV

    Paste your CV as plain text. It powers fit scores, tailored CVs, cover letters and interview prep. Headings like "## Experience" and "- " bullets help, but plain text works too.

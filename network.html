Referrals & LinkedIn posts

Two ways to get ahead of the job boards: ask someone inside for a referral, and catch roles that are only announced in LinkedIn posts. Jobber never logs in to LinkedIn and never messages anyone for you.

Add jobs from a LinkedIn post

Copy a post's text (a list of job links, or a recruiter's "we're hiring") and paste it here. Jobber follows each link to the real job page, keeps the roles that match your search and remembers who posted it.

Find hiring posts automatically

Every time you press Find new jobs, Jobber runs a few web searches for public LinkedIn posts that mention your roles and cities, then reads the jobs in them. It only sees posts that are public on the web.

Searches Jobber will run

Built from your roles and cities in Setup. Fine-tune the words in config.json → network.

Better results: add a free search key (optional)

Without a key Jobber uses DuckDuckGo, which works but sometimes limits automated searches. A free Brave Search API key (about 2,000 searches a month) is more reliable. A Google Programmable Search key and engine ID also work.

Stored only in secrets.json on this computer.

How referrals work in Jobber

  1. Every job has a "Get a referral" box. Open a job on the Jobs page: one click opens LinkedIn people search for that company, filtered to your 1st and 2nd-degree connections, plus alumni and ex-colleague searches when your school and last company are in Setup.
  2. A referral message is drafted for you. Copy it, add their name, and send it yourself on LinkedIn.
  3. Jobs where you know someone move up. Tick "I know someone here" on a job, or let the Chrome extension notice LinkedIn's own "N connections work here" line when you open a LinkedIn job page.
Track who you asked in Copilot → Outreach.

With the Chrome extension

  1. Save jobs from a post: while reading LinkedIn, scroll a hiring post to the middle of your screen (or select its text), click the Jobber extension icon and press Save jobs from this post.
  2. Connection boost: when you open any LinkedIn job page, the extension reads the "connections work here" note LinkedIn already shows you and tells Jobber. It never clicks, scrolls or visits anything on its own.
Install or update it: chrome://extensions → Developer mode → Load unpacked → the chrome-extension folder (or press reload on its card).
